# RFC-0039: Datagram Adaptive FEC

- Number: RFC-0039
- Status: Implemented
- Created: 2026-08-21
- Last updated: 2026-08-24

## Background and Problem

Physical datagram has no stream-level retransmission. When there is short-term packet loss or sudden
packet loss on the link, a single end-to-end frame will be lost directly, and recovery can only wait
for the upper layer to retry. An optional, limited, link-by-link FEC layer is required without
compromising end-to-end encryption and relay transparency.

## Goals and Non-goals

Target:

- Perform sliding window linear encoding on the complete end-to-end datagram.
- Estimate packet loss and dynamically select the repair rate through reliable feedback from the
  receiver.
- Allow configuration to limit the upper limit of repair/source, which is turned off by default.
- Properly reduces MTU when enabled and does not increase Forward packet space when disabled.

Non-goals:

- Does not replace Circuit's reliable control flow or lifecycle feedback.
- Do not let the relay decrypt the end-to-end payload.
- End-to-end FEC semantics across Links are not guaranteed.

## Configuration and negotiation

The top-level `repair_rate` is the upper limit of the passive side; `repair_rate` in the
`connect_to` object is the upper limit of the active side. The active value in the old string form
is zero. The final upper limit of Link is the smaller value of both sides, ranging from `0..=8`; the
non-zero configuration is at least `0.001`, expressed as a certain number of points in one
thousandth on the wire. Hello/Ack only takes optional extensions when non-zero; explicit
`repair_rate_milli=0` is rejected, and no handshake or data fields are added when both parties
close.

## algorithm

The standalone FEC module uses GF(256) and a maximum 256 symbol window. The source symbol is
`[frame_len:u16 | frame]` and the source packet is sent as is. The repair packet uses linear
combinations of deterministic random non-zero coefficients of the most recent up to 64 source
symbols. The coefficients are generated by a fixed 32-bit blending function of repair id and window
index, and are locked by a fixed vector test. The decoder uses bounded row minimum elimination with
a fixed 512 slots. `Delivery` clearly distinguishes source and recovered frames, and supports
out-of-order, repetition, wraparound, window elimination and symbols of different lengths; expired
symbols are only generated once `Lost` and will not block the receive cycle. The source path updates
the decoder in place after completing input verification; repair still maintains transaction
semantics, but shares the large buffer of slot/equation to avoid copying the complete window for
each packet.

The adaptive controller uses a 64-symbol reference window, target residual loss rate `1e-3`, minimum
coded loss rate `0.005`, and window chaining factor `2.5`. Repair credit is accumulated using whole
thousandths, and the long-term average will not exceed the negotiated upper limit; the sender and
receiver share the credit upper limit of 512 repairs. The sender implements this upper limit when
the source is credited and when local unsent repair is recovered, and the excess backlog of credits
is discarded to avoid generating an unbounded repair burst at one time after continuous backpressure
recovery. Maintain the latest legal rate when feedback is invalid, duplicate, or late. The adaptive
controller treats `FecReport` as a measurement with confidence rather than a direct command: the
report requires at least 128 samples, and `loss_ppm == 1_000_000` indicates that there are no
arriving samples that can be used to estimate parity. The current rate is maintained and handed over
to Link health/recovery for processing, without directly increasing the repair rate to the upper
limit; other reports with illegal formats or insufficient samples also do not change the current
rate. The effective target rate uses a per-thousandth slope limit of up to 1.0 increments and 0.5
increments to avoid parity mutations caused by a single feedback window; the negotiated value is
still only the final upper limit. Data plane health and bearer layer health are recorded separately,
but routes are exposed as the same Link health: only three consecutive valid saturated packet loss
reports will mark the Link as unhealthy and request Circuit Reconcile; a single burst, insufficient
samples, illegal reports, and no subsequent reports will not change the state. After downgrading,
two consecutive valid non-saturated reports are required to recover. During this period, the
successful sending of ordinary datagram cannot clear the FEC downgrade. To prevent the only first
hop from being unable to recover due to the route snapshot skipping unhealthy binding, Link
independently sends a fixed-size Link-local recovery probe batch during FEC degradation; the probe
uses a physical authenticated FEC source, and the receiver only lets it enter the loss estimator
without publishing it to the Forward queue, and continues to feedback reports through reliable
control flow. This mechanism only handles evidence of "a small number of FEC packets still arriving
but the window is saturated"; the absence of FEC packets at all is still the responsibility of
bearer layer keepalives, connection closures, and other recovery mechanisms.

For repair-first delivery, the decoder first inverts the actual coefficient of the singleton row and
then releases the source; elimination, recovery and source delivery are completed in the candidate
decoder state, and no row, slot or delivery result is submitted when the recovery vector is
illegal. When receiving a legal ESI that far exceeds the current window, clear the old window and
establish a fixed-width window with the new ESI. Do not treat `RepairWindowJump` as a permanently
rejected state; the window size and the state generated by a reset are both bounded.

## physical format

The FEC source packet uses an independent physical form and adds `transmission_sequence:u32 |
esi:u32` before the existing split forward envelope; this prefix goes into the Physical AEAD AAD.
The repair packet uses the complete Physical AEAD, and the authentication plaintext is
`transmission_sequence:u32 | rid:u32 | first_esi:u32 | symbol_count:u16 | repair_vector`. Illegal
counts, empty vectors, out-of-bounds windows, and overlong vectors are rejected before entering the
decoder.

After authentication, the receiver sends the source packet and repair packet to the decoder of this
Link, and only releases the complete end-to-end frame to the dataplane. The recovery frame first
enters the ready queue with a fixed capacity of the same magnitude as the Forward single consumer
queue; when the ready queue is full, it is discarded and included in the local
`forward_queue_drops`, and must not be backlogged indefinitely. After relay receives the complete
frame of a Link, it re-executes FEC encapsulation on the next Link.

## Feedback and convergence

The receiver uses the transmission sequence window of slot 1024 to delay acknowledgment of packet
loss based on the reorder tolerance of 8 sequence numbers; late packets are only counted in the
reorder, and confirmed statistics are not written back. If a high sequence number is received first
and then a lower sequence number is received during the startup phase, the current lower sequence
number must be marked as arrived before determining the gap in the window. Authentication failures
and packets with illegal formats are not included in FEC packet loss. The transmission sequence is
uniformly allocated by the local FEC sender for source and repair, and is accompanied by Physical
AEAD authentication; repair stops this batch when local admission, backpressure or MTU check fails.
Unsent repair sequence numbers must be recycled, and false path packet loss cannot be caused in the
receiver. The estimator only observes FEC source/repair sequences with successful authentication and
does not read QUIC ACK, QUIC loss, RTT, congestion window or path statistics, so QUIC DATAGRAM,
bare UDP and other datagram bearers use the same statistical semantics. After the source is
successful, if there is no new source within a bounded idle grace period, the sender will only try
tail repair for the source symbol of the current burst; the number of tails is limited by the
current burst length and the sender's accumulated repair credit. No additional credit will be
created, and the entire window repair will not be sent because the sliding window is full. If there
is not enough credit, only burst will be closed this time and repair will not be sent. Each source
burst only triggers tail once; tail retains unsent credits after local backpressure or admission
rejection, and retries with bounded backoff to ensure continuous transmission sequence and long-term
repair upper limit. After reaching the bounded sample size, a monotonic report id of `FecReport` is
generated, which is sent through an independent reliable control flow worker and does not depend on
whether the Forward is received later. Each Link only retains the latest unsent report; when sending
fails, it continues to retry with a bounded backoff of 50ms to 2s until the Link is closed. The
sender only accepts larger report ids and clips the calculation results to the configured upper
limit. The receiver uses an independent token bucket for repair: it supplements the credit
corresponding to the negotiation rate based on the source ESI covered by the repair, does not exceed
the negotiation upper limit in the long term, and limits the burst credit to 512 repairs. The
decoder first checks whether the repair is still within the window in read-only mode; repairs that
are expired or will cause the window to cross the boundary do not consume credit and do not enter
the decoder, and are recorded as discarded. After passing the window check, the decoder is executed
with the candidate status; credit is submitted only when the decoder actually accepts the equation,
and the equation discarded by the decoder rolls back the candidate status. Excess repair does not
enter the decoder and is recorded separately as rate-limited, without being disguised as path packet
loss. A single source batch of the sender cannot exceed the credit limit; if local admission,
backpressure or capacity fails, only the unsent part will be recycled and trimmed again by the upper
limit. The FEC health edge is rebuilt using the `CircuitOpenManager::request_reconcile()` wakeup
routing snapshot of the same Link, and is verified with the Link instance to avoid late feedback
from the old connection contaminating the replaced Link.

`fec_rx_sources`, `fec_rx_repairs`, `fec_recovered`, `fec_lost`, `fec_discarded`,
`fec_rate_limited`, `fec_reordered`, `fec_invalid` in state respectively represent the received
source, repair, and the real one restored by repair Frame, window confirmed lost source, dropped
delivery, repair rejected by admission, out-of-order packets and illegal FEC packets; `fec_loss_ppm`
and `fec_loss_samples` are the latest reports generated by the local transmission sequence
estimator, not the packet loss rate of the underlying bearer; `fec_feedback_sent` and
`fec_feedback_retries` represent the number of successful transmissions and failed retries of the
independent feedback worker. `fec_recovered` does not include direct source delivery. LinkMetrics
does not expose Quinn's RTT, ACK loss, congestion window, path MTU, or send buffer metrics;
`datagram_max_size` only represents the current physical payload capabilities provided by the
carrier abstraction.

After the source is successfully sent, the ESI and transmission sequence state of the sender are
submitted. Repair is a best-effort transmission: when local admission is rejected, queue back
pressure or the current capacity is insufficient, the current repair batch can be stopped, and the
source batch is still successful; the transmission sequence that has not sent repair must be
recycled, and other fatal carrier errors are returned to the caller, but the source that has been
sent must not be rolled back. The carrier clearly divides the sending results into Accepted, local
rejection and link failure; QUIC typed `TooLarge` is a local rejection, and the Link cannot be
marked as unhealthy due to the PMTU change between preflight and actual sending. Real connection
errors such as `ConnectionLost` still trigger link recovery. The FEC transmission sequence can be
rolled back when the transmission is not accepted, but the PhysicalSession sequence number used for
Physical AEAD encapsulation remains monotonically increasing.

Link rebuilding discards old decoder, report, ESI/RID, and transmission sequence state to prevent
control feedback or repair equations from the old session from contaminating the new session.

## MTU, Security and Compatibility

Ordinary user IP capacity is deducted by 66 bytes; FEC Link is deducted by repair in the worst case
of 82 bytes. The source packet actually adds 8 bytes, and the repair packet is the most stringent
capacity constraint. Continue to use the original `FORM_FORWARD` and normal capacity conversion when
turning off FEC.

Both FEC metadata and repair vector are protected by Physical AEAD; the source packet's clear prefix
goes into AAD. The Replay window is still bound to the existing Physical sequence. FEC does not
change the end-to-end ciphertext, Circuit authorization, or `EgressPolicy::authorize()` boundaries.
This protocol change requires communication nodes to be upgraded at the same time.

## Testing and Acceptance

- GF(256), coefficients, window elimination, shuffle/repeat/wrap and different length source
  symbols.
- Recovery after source packet loss, repair loss, illegal repair and AAD tampering.
- Configuration compatibility, rate clipping, feedback deduplication and MTU-accurate values
    with FEC switch.
- Direct link, multi-hop relay, link reconstruction and control flow feedback anomalies.
- repair-first exact recovery, illegal recovery transaction rollback, large jump window reset,
  startup out-of-order estimation, and send semantics where source succeeds but repair fails under
  backpressure.
- State metrics for QUIC DATAGRAM and bare UDP are derived from local FEC sequences and carrier
  counts; state binary encoding and decoding and Prometheus output must not be reintroduced into
  Quinn path statistics.
- The feedback worker can still send and retry independently in direct link, multi-hop relay,
  two-hop packet loss/out of order, downstream restart or Link replacement, and when there is no
  subsequent Forward.
- Tail repair of short source burst (limited by burst length and credit), tail repair rollback and
  continuous transmission sequence after local admission failure, bounded FEC ready queue, and
  decoder source/repair hot path do not copy the complete symbol/equation buffer.
- When there is continuous repair back pressure or capacity rejection, the sender credit and the
  number of repairs in a single batch after recovery remain bounded, and the serial numbers of
  unsent repairs can still be continuously recycled.
- When QUIC preflight passes but `SendDatagram` actually returns typed `TooLarge`, the FEC state
  of source/repair/tail is rolled back, the Link health does not change, and `ConnectionLost` is
  still regarded as a link failure; 100% loss, insufficient samples, and invalid burst reports
  maintain the latest legal rate, and the rate increase or decrease obeys the slope limit.
- Continuous saturated packet loss reports trigger Link health degradation, route reconciliation
  and continuous valid report recovery; repeated/late/illegal/insufficient sample feedback does
  not advance the health lag status, and ordinary datagrams successfully sent do not bypass FEC
  degradation.
- After the only first hop in the route snapshot skips unhealthy binding, the Link-local recovery
  probe can still independently generate FEC feedback; the probe does not enter the secure/relay
  forward queue, and bounded stops when Link is replaced or the connection is closed.
- `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy --all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings`, `cargo test`
  and privileged network fault injection testing.

- `src/physical.rs` / `src/carrier/mod.rs`: physical format, negotiation, Link state and
  capacity.
- `src/control.rs`:Link-local FEC feedback.
- `src/mtu.rs`: Normal/FEC capacity conversion.
- `src/carrier/mod.rs`: carrier sending result classification, FEC rate controller application and
  Link health aggregation.
- `src/daemon/control_handler.rs` / `src/daemon/physical_dispatch.rs` / `src/dataplane.rs` /
  `src/main.rs`: FEC health edge reconcile access, recovery probe, state synchronization and
